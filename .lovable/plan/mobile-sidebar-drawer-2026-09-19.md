# Mobile sidebar drawer

## What will change
- Turn the sidebar into an off-canvas drawer on phone-sized screens, layered above the page with a dimmed backdrop.
- Keep the desktop collapsible icon strip unchanged.
- Close the drawer after any navigation choice, when tapping the backdrop, when pressing Escape, or when the route changes.
- Prevent the page behind the open drawer from scrolling and retain clear open/close controls.

## Verification
- Check the drawer at the current 440 × 807 mobile viewport.
- Confirm it opens above the content, navigation closes it, and backdrop/Escape dismissal works.
- Confirm the desktop sidebar still collapses and expands normally.
